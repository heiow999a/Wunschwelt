Frequently asked questions

Your question isn’t here? Just write to us.

Do you need my password?

No. You invite us as staff in your Shopify admin and only grant access to the areas the job needs. Once we’re done, you can remove the access again.

Does this work with my theme?

We work with Online Store 2.0 themes like Dawn, Sense or Impulse and most premium themes. If something can’t be done cleanly with your theme, we tell you before the quote.

What if I don’t like the result?

Every package includes one revision round. Since changes are made in a copy first, nothing goes live until you’re happy.

Why no phone calls?

Written coordination is more precise. Requirements, prices and dates are documented, and you don’t have to squeeze a call into your day.

How do I pay?

You receive an invoice by email once the work is done. For larger projects, a 50% deposit is agreed.

Tell us what your store needs.

Write to us via the form or directly by email. You’ll get a fixed-price quote within 2 business days.
